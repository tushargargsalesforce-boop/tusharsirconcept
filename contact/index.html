Contact Talkifi

For safety, legal, privacy, or platform concerns, contact the Talkifi operator through the monitored address used by this website.

Safety reports

Use the Report button during an active chat for harassment, scams, threats, sexual or explicit content, hate or abuse, impersonation, or suspicious behavior.

Privacy requests

Include only the temporary session identifier shown by your browser if you have it. Never include passwords, OTPs, banking details, or sensitive documents.

Legal and general contact

Send your concern to talk@thewebsite. For urgent danger, contact local emergency services.

See Grievance and complaints for escalation information.
