Community Guidelines

Keep Talkifi respectful. Use Report or Block in an active chat whenever someone crosses a line.

Harassment and hate

Do not threaten, bully, intimidate, repeatedly harass, or target anyone with abusive or hateful content.

Sexual exploitation and minors

Sexual exploitation, coercion, trafficking, or exploitation of any person is prohibited. Any sexual or exploitative interaction involving a minor is strictly prohibited.

Consent and private content

Do not share another person's private, intimate, or sexual material without their consent. Do not record or distribute conversations without permission.

Scams and impersonation

Do not deceive users, request money fraudulently, send malicious links, or pretend to be another person or organization.

Enforcement

Reports can be reviewed and may result in warnings, restrictions, blocking, content removal, or access removal.

Submit a grievance or complaint.
