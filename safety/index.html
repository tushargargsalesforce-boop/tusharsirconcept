Safety First

Talkifi cannot verify another user's identity, intentions, or behavior. Make careful choices online and offline.

During a chat

  • Do not share passwords, OTPs, banking information, addresses, or personal documents.
  • Never send money to someone you meet online.
  • Use Report and Block if someone makes you uncomfortable, threatens you, or asks for sensitive information.

Meeting offline

  • Meet in a public place.
  • Tell someone you trust where you are going.
  • Arrange your own transport and leave if anything feels unsafe.

Users are responsible for their own decisions about meeting other users offline.

In immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
